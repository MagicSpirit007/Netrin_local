# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Netrin_local a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples include showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and
accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and
focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, harassment, publishing another person's private information (including
location or photographs) without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in
a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's
GitHub profile or, when the matter also presents a security or privacy risk,
through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not disclose
sensitive reports in a public Issue.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will
be handled as privately and fairly as practical.
